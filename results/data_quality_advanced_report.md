## Methodology

- **Class discriminability**: Fisher's LDR and Mann-Whitney AUROC on mu+beta band power per channel
- **Temporal drift**: L2 distance of per-run channel mean vectors from first run in each session
- **Spectral features**: Welch PSD (nperseg=100, up to 500-trial subsample)
- **EMG indicators**: High-frequency (20-40 Hz) power ratio, peripheral vs central (motor cortex 32ch) channels
- **Adjacent trial correlation**: Pearson r of channel-mean vectors between consecutive trials within runs
- **Cross-subject similarity**: Euclidean distance in z-scored 10-feature space
- **Central region**: Motor cortex 32ch configuration (C3/Cz/C4 + SMA/premotor, 32 channels)
- **Peripheral region**: Remaining 96 channels
